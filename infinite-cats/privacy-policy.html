Privacy Policy — Infinite Cats

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Infinite Cats is developed by quentin452 ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what the app collects, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

Contact: catzworld.contact@gmail.com

The short version

The game itself does not collect anything about you. Your save stays on your device, and never reaches us — though Android's own backup service may copy it to your Google account, as it does for any app. Two third parties embedded in the app — Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (purchases) — do collect data, and that is the whole of it.

What the app stores on your device

Your progress (fish, cats, prestige, upgrades, settings, purchase entitlements) is written to your device's local storage. We have no servers and no account system, so it is never sent to us and we cannot see it.

Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account. If you have device backup switched on, Android copies app data — including this save — to Google Drive, and restores it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. That is Google's service acting on your device, not ours: we neither send it nor receive it, and it is covered by Google's privacy policy and by your device's backup settings, where you can turn it off. The app's log and gameplay-diagnostic files are excluded from it. Uninstalling the app removes the save from the device; a copy may remain in your Google backup until you delete it there.

What third parties collect

Google AdMob shows the banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To do that it may collect a device advertising identifier, IP address, an approximate location derived from that address, and coarse information about your device and ad interactions, and may use it to personalise ads. Its software also reports its own crashes and diagnostics to Google. We do not receive any of it in identifiable form — only aggregate counts of impressions and revenue.

  • Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked FIRST. On the first launch the app shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform, a certified CMP) and requests no ad at all until you have answered it. Refusing personalisation does not change the game in any way.
  • You can change your answer at any time: Settings → Ad privacy options reopens the same form. That entry appears only where the choice applies.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" stops banner and interstitial ads; the optional rewarded videos remain available if you choose to watch one.

Google Play Billing processes in-app purchases. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, name or address. We receive only whether a purchase completed, so the app can unlock what you bought.

  • Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules local reminders that your cats have been fishing. They are generated on your device — nothing is sent anywhere. Denying the permission, or turning reminders off in the app's settings, stops them.

Children

Infinite Cats is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ad network, contact us and we will help you direct the request to Google, who holds the data.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. Data held by Google is subject to Google's own policies, linked above, and to the controls in your Google account and device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version replaces this page at the same URL.
